COMPANY PROFILE: HDFC Bank Limited
Industry: Banking & Financial Services (India)

BUSINESS OVERVIEW:
HDFC Bank is India's largest private sector bank by assets and market capitalization. 
The bank serves retail, SME, and corporate customers through an omnichannel model 
combining digital banking, branch network, and third-party distribution.

VALUE PROPOSITION:
Trusted financial partner delivering seamless digital-first banking experiences with 
personalized products across savings, lending, investments, and insurance.

KEY PRODUCTS & SERVICES:
- Retail Banking: Savings accounts, fixed deposits, home loans, personal loans, credit cards
- Corporate Banking: Working capital finance, trade finance, treasury services
- SME Banking: Business loans, current accounts, cash management
- Wealth Management: Mutual funds, insurance, portfolio management
- Digital Banking: NetBanking, MobileBanking, PayZapp, SmartBUY

REVENUE STREAMS:
- Net Interest Income (NII) from loan-deposit spread
- Fee income from transaction banking, forex, and advisory
- Insurance and wealth distribution commissions
- Credit card annual fees and interchange

STRATEGIC PRIORITIES:
1. Accelerate digital adoption (75% digital transactions target)
2. Expand rural and semi-urban footprint
3. Cross-sell wealth & insurance products to existing base
4. Strengthen cybersecurity and regulatory compliance

DISTRIBUTION:
- 7,800+ branches, 19,000+ ATMs
- Digital channels (app, netbanking, API banking)
- Business Correspondents in rural markets
- Partnership with NBFCs, fintechs, and insurance companies

COMPETITIVE POSITIONING:
Premium service, technology leadership, and risk management discipline vs PSU banks.
Competes with ICICI Bank, Axis Bank, SBI in key segments.
